A metaphor of the global state¶

Taking advantage of the visit to our universe, the engineer SysT+ has received an additional task: to count how many smart civilizations are there in our galaxy. SysT+, which in his random transition of the universe has casually stopped on our planet, feels very optimistic that it has hit first with a species that, in some sense, could almost be considered intelligent. In addition, SysT+ has an ultra-fast ship to explore the galaxy.

What will SysT+ think when he finds out that in our galaxy there are more than 100,000 million solar systems scattered on a disk with a diameter of 100,000 light years, that the average life of an intelligent civilization probably does not reach one million years, and, what is more serious, that in our universe Einstein’s Law of Relativity prohibits driving at a speed greater than that of light?
