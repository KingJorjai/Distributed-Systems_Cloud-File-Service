A metaphor of causality¶

The SysT+ engineer, a member of an ultra-advanced civilization of the Oderguorld parallel universe, has been sent to our universe through a technology incomprehensible to us with the mission of studying our physical laws, presumably very different from their own. The coincidence makes SysT+ suddenly appear precisely on our planet, at the stadium where the judge is about to start the 100 meters Olympic final. SysT+, after a moment of logical stunning, is ready to prepare a report on what is happening. Here is the translation of an excerpt from his report:

I have been able to verify how eight of the beings of the species that seems to be the dominant in this planet were standing up simultaneously to undertake a race in the same direction at high speed. A fraction of a second (the second is the unit of measurement of time in this region of the universe) after the stampede I heard how an individual of the same species, located very close to the eight bipeds, used what they call here a “weapon of fire” (which as I could see serves to eliminate other individuals). Fortunately, there were no casualties. One of the hypotheses that I consider is that these beings have some organ or technology that allows them to synchronize perfectly. They can also predict the future, which allowed them to flee to avoid being hit by the shot. However, I must still analyze some physical laws that could provide another explanation for these phenomena.

Let’s help SysT+ a little. In our universe, phenomena are explained in terms of causal relationships. A cause always happens earlier in time than its effect. In addition, time always goes the same direction. However, physical laws can confuse us in the perception of events. Knowing the physical laws, we can place the events in their correct time scale.
