A defalcation in the global state¶

The Sociedad Informática Contra la Corrupción (SINCOCO) is dedicated to analyzing information from political parties, large construction companies and banking entities with the aim of collecting clues that allow it to discover various frauds and scams. SINCOCO currently suspects that part of the funds allocated to the works of a city infrastructure are being diverted.

SINCOCO intends to discover if the amount of the balances of the accounts of the fund destined for the works, A, of the bank that manages the payments, B, and of the construction company, C, remains invariant or if on the contrary part of the money “is lost” in the payment process.

Initially in A a budget of 100 million is established. The managers of A issue payments to B as a provision of funds, while B pays C according to the progress of the works. The final cost of the works cannot exceed 100 million and the remaining money must remain entirely in A. The bank does not charge any commission.

The local government issues an account report:

The final cost of the works has been 85,000,000. The payments made by the local Administration through the Bank are that same amount. Since the initial budget was 100,000,000, the operation shows a surplus of 15,000,000 for the Administration. The statements of the accounts as of 30/05/2019 are attached as proof of operations:

CUENTA A:
   11/01/2019: SALDO INICIAL             100.000.000
   17/02/2019: TRANSFERENCIA A CUENTA B  -50.000.000
   21/03/2019: TRANSFERENCIA A CUENTA B  -35.000.000
   30/05/2019: SALDO A 30/05/2019         15.000.000
CUENTA B:
   18/02/2019: INGRESO POR TRANSFERENCIA  50.000.000
   19/02/2019: TRANSFERENCIA A CUENTA C  -50.000.000
   21/03/2019: INGRESO POR TRANSFERENCIA  35.000.000
   25/04/2019: TRANSFERENCIA A CUENTA C  -35.000.000
   30/05/2019: INGRESO POR TRANSFERENCIA   5.000.000
   30/05/2019: TRANSFERENCIA A CUENTA X   -5.000.000
   30/05/2019: SALDO A 30/05/2019                  0
CUENTA C:
   20/02/2019: INGRESO POR TRANSFERENCIA  50.000.000
   28/04/2019: INGRESO POR TRANSFERENCIA  35.000.000
   30/05/2019: SALDO A 30/05/2019         85.000.000

SINCOCO suspects that the Construction Company could have paid a commission of 5,000,000 to be awarded the works, which would end up in the hands of party members in the local government. However, SINCOCO computer scientists are not sure how to gather evidence solid enough for the judge. Fortunately, one of them is a Distributed Systems student and believes he can apply the techniques he is learning.
